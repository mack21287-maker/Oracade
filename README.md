# Oracade

**AI research and products that make health and learning human.**

Oracade is the laboratory and parent company behind a family of AI
products. Our flagship brand is **Fitro** — an AI fitness trainer and
medical study companion that answers one analogy first, then at full
clinical depth.

The relationship is the same shape as Anthropic and Claude: Oracade is
the company, Fitro is the product.
